Project Wuming / Week 2 greybox exploration candidate

Keep ProjectWuming.exe and ProjectWuming.pck together.
Launch ProjectWuming.exe, choose Enter town.
WASD/arrows: move. Shift: sprint. E/Enter: interact or close reading.
Hold F: observe nearby objects. Esc: close reading or pause/back.
F5: quick save. Pause menu: separate manual save slots, load and fullscreen.
Manual slots 1-5 are independent of quick save. Confirm before overwriting.
Title and pause Load menus let you select a slot. Automatic triggers are not implemented yet.
Explore town, home courtyard and river dock through marked exits.
Save data: %APPDATA%\ProjectWuming\saves\quick.sav
No Steam client or login required.

Keyboard is the current acceptance baseline. Controller mappings remain,
but physical controller compatibility is not yet verified or promised.
Windows hardware acceptance still needs actual PC testing: graphics,
keyboard exploration, save, quit/reopen/load, and fullscreen.
Record OS/GPU/input device/build_info.json and each result.
This is a greybox prototype: no finished story, combat or production art.
